Meet Whitney's digital team

Cooper is Whitney's chief of staff. Cooper works with Booker, Knox and eight directors. A director can have sidekicks: smaller agents that each do one narrow job for that director. Together they are the agentic workforce Whitney built and runs.

Select any name to see its purpose, the model it runs on and what it is accountable for.

As of October 2026. Models change. Opus, Sonnet and Haiku are families of AI models from Anthropic.

The jobs that keep it running

These run on a schedule. The agents draft, route and check, and Whitney makes the final call.

  • Every night

    Memory consolidation

    Runs as Booker. Keeps the workforce's shared memory in order.

  • Every night

    Crew check

    Notes which agents have been active.

  • Every morning

    Morning briefings

    Whitney gets a briefing on the day ahead, a status note for each active project and a back-office list.

  • Every morning

    Briefing check

    Confirms the morning briefing went out.

  • Every day

    Email triage

    Sorts open loops into what Whitney owes and what she is waiting on.

  • Regularly

    Backup

    The workforce's files are backed up regularly.

  • Sunday evenings

    Weekly reset

    Runs as Cooper. Tidies the week's lists.

  • Every day

    Filing reminder

    Runs the annual-filing-reminder. It speaks up only when a filing is due.
