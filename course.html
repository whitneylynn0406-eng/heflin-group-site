Who It's For · What's Covered

Built for people, not just programmers.

No prior AI experience, no programming background. If you can describe what you want in plain English, you can build it.

Two days, module by module — taught, demonstrated live, then built by you on your own Claude Code subscription (Pro or Max).

Day 1 — Build the assistant

  • The reframe. Get your own machine live, and go from chatting with AI to a system that knows your world.
  • Your boot file. The one file that turns a generic assistant into yours.
  • Context & memory. Teach it your real world, so it's right between sessions.
  • The permission model. AI is probabilistic — this is how you make it deterministic where it counts, so you get the results you want and prevent the ones you don't.

Day 2 — From assistant to workforce

  • Your first skill. A reusable move for what you do every week.
  • Your first agent. A working "Morning Brief," built live.
  • Memory, maintenance & orchestration. How a growing workforce stays in sync instead of drifting.
  • Back it up. Automatic backups so you never lose everything you've built — plus a simple plan for what to build next.

Building along? A laptop (Windows or Mac) with admin rights — bring a real task, sanitized first (no client, patient, or proprietary data; use sample data). Just watching? No setup — show up and follow along.

By the end, you've built the two parts every AI worker runs on — an agent, and the skill it reaches for — and seen how a whole roster of them stays in sync instead of drifting. You leave with the templates. Then you keep building.
